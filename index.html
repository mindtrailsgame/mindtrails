Welcome to Mind Trails

This is a card-based reflection game played in pairs. One person is the Seeker, and the other is the Guide.

The Seeker chooses a challenge or goal. Together, you will walk through 6 card stages — each helping the Seeker explore, reframe, and move forward.

How It Works:

  1. Start with "Goal" cards to define a challenge.
  2. Read the question aloud and reflect together.
  3. When you're ready, continue to the next category.

Context
Lukas is stuck at work — unsure whether to pursue a promotion or switch careers.
Conversation Flow
Goal (Change)
Anna: “If you could change one thing about your life right now, what would it be?”
Lukas: “To stop feeling stuck and be excited about my work again, whether it's here or somewhere new.”
Support (Overcome)
Anna: “Recall a difficult situation you successfully navigated. What skills did you use then?”
Lukas: “Moving to a new city for college. I had to be brave and take a leap of faith, even though I was scared.”
Exploration (Motivation)
Anna: “Deep down, why is achieving this goal important to you right now?”
Lukas: “It’s about my personal growth. I feel like I’m coasting, and I want to feel challenged again.”
Challenge (Fear)
Anna: “What fear holds you back from making progress?”
Lukas: “The fear of the unknown, and of disappointing my current boss if I explore other options.”
Creativity (No-Fear)
Anna: “If you knew you could not fail, what new or brave step would you take?”
Lukas: “I’d ask for a 3-month sabbatical to explore a passion project without having to quit my job entirely.”
Action (First-Step)
Anna: “What is one clear step you can take this week?”
Lukas: “I’ll draft a proposal for that sabbatical project. It feels less scary than just quitting.”
Closing
Anna: “Looking back — what surprised you most? What do you want to carry forward?”

Controls ⚙️

  • Flip Card: Click on any card to flip it over and see the question.
  • See All Words: Click and hold on any card to see all the clarifying words for that category.
  • Navigate Categories: Use the ⬅️ and ➡️ arrows at the bottom of the screen.
  • Complete Category: Click the "Complete" button to unlock the next category. The game will remember your progress.
  • Restart Game: Click the 🔄 icon at the top-left to start over.
